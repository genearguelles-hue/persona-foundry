# Persona Foundry Privacy Policy

Effective Date:** May 10, 2026 
Developer: Gene Arguelles / Gene Arguelles LLC  
Contact: geneaerguelles@me.com

Persona Foundry is an iOS application for creating, saving, exporting, and running structured AI persona specifications. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may store or transmit and how that information is used.

## Information Stored Locally

Persona Foundry stores saved persona specifications locally on your device. These persona specifications may include:

- Persona name
- Mission type and mission description
- Core identity text
- Persona axioms
- Persona primitives
- Engram schema
- Avatar prompt
- System prompt
- Generated timestamps

This data is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app’s core persona creation, library, review, export, and runtime features.

## Information Sent to the Backend

When you use the “Run Persona” feature, Persona Foundry may send the selected persona specification and your generation request to a configured backend service. This may include:

- The selected persona specification
- Your prompt or post intent
- Generation settings such as platform, style, and maximum word count

This information is used to generate persona-governed draft content.

## Third-Party AI Processing

The backend service may send generation requests to an AI model provider, such as OpenAI, to produce draft content. These requests may include the persona specification and the user-provided generation prompt.

Do not enter highly sensitive personal information, confidential business information, regulated data, or emergency/crisis information into Persona Foundry.

## Generated Content

Generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate for some uses. You are responsible for reviewing generated content before relying on it, publishing it, or sharing it.

Persona Foundry is not a medical, mental health, legal, financial, or professional advice service.

## Data Deletion

You can delete locally saved personas from within the app using the Settings screen. Deleting local personas removes the saved persona data from the device.

Data sent to a backend service or third-party AI provider may be subject to the backend provider’s and AI provider’s data handling policies.

## Children’s Privacy

Persona Foundry is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
